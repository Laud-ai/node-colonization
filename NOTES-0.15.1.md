# 0.15.1 — Designer review: small flags, fervor cue

Designer review of the asset list and the 0.15.0 flags; Tiago's choices stand. Presentation only; no rule changes.

- **Fervor ring non-colour cue:** the ring around each pin is split into 3 segments — 1, 2 or 3 lit as fervor nears the independence threshold — plus a dashed outer ring when critical (≥90%). Colour stays as a second cue.
- **Small flags (≤30px wide: route labels, map strip, card icons):** 5 stripes instead of 13, a simple union (cross + saltire), a 6-dot ring instead of the star ring, and no specialty symbol.
- **Lighter flag keyline** (#5A6B7D) so dark flags stand out on the dark UI.
- **Multinational blocs no longer copy one country:** East Asian Pacific Alliance disc gets a blue border (not Japan); African Union, Latin Federation and South Asian Commonwealth carry a ring of stars (not Ghana, Colombia or India); the Commonwealth ensign adds a star.
- Stars-and-stripes and ensign flags carry at most one extra charge; gold on saffron is never generated.
- Asset list (`out/reports/visual-overhaul-assets.md`) gained the designer's accessibility and polish rows (list only, no reskin).
